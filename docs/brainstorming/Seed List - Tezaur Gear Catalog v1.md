# Seed List - Tezaur Gear Catalog v1

> Seed catalog for Sintezaur's "Tezaur" (gear encyclopedia). Hand-curated for the Romanian/Eastern-European synth scene at launch.
> Targets: synth-heavy (~50), with drum machines, eurorack, samplers, effects, MIDI controllers, interfaces, and a long-tail sampler.
> Price estimates in EUR are best-effort approximations of MSRP / current street at launch year — verify per entry before publishing.
> Status note: `year_discontinued` empty = still in production as of 2026.

## Distribution (final tally)

- **Synthesizers**: 52 (covers analog mono/poly/paraphonic, FM, VA, wavetable, hybrid, digital, drone)
- **Drum machines**: 10 (TR-808/909 vintage + TR-8S/6S + Behringer clones + Elektron Rytm + Korg/TE budget)
- **Eurorack modules**: 9 + 2 cases = 11 (Plaits/Maths/Marbles/Rings/Beads/Disting EX/Mimeophon/Quadrax/Doepfer + Mantis/Pod)
- **Samplers / grooveboxes**: 8 (MPC Live II, MPC One+, Maschine+, Digitakt II, Octatrack MkII, Model:Samples, Volca Sample 2, Circuit Tracks)
- **Effects (hardware pedals)**: 5 (BigSky MX, TimeLine, H90, Empress Reverb, Mood MKII)
- **MIDI controllers**: 5 (KeyStep Pro, KeyStep, Launchpad Pro MK3, Launchkey 49 MK3, MPK Mini MK3, Push 3) — actually 6
- **Audio interfaces**: 6 (Apollo Twin X Duo, Apollo x4, Scarlett 2i2/4i4, Babyface Pro FS, MOTU M4)
- **Long tail** (mixers/recorders/monitors/headphones/mics): 11 (ProFX12v3, Xone:96, Portacapture X8, H6essential, HS8, T7V, Rokit 5, DT 770 Pro, ATH-M50x, HD 650, SM57, SM7B)

**Total: ~108 entries** — slightly above 100 because the long-tail row count grew while writing. Iulian can trim to ~80 by cutting headphones/mics down to 2 each and removing 2–3 of the older boutique-tier vintage entries if needed.
